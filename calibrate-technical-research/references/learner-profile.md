# Learner Profile

Use this file as a conservative starting point, not as ground truth. Prefer explicit statements in the current conversation.

## Confirmed Background

- The learner has some reinforcement-learning algorithm background.
- The learner can follow technical mechanisms and does not need every settled definition supported by a paper.
- The learner is less familiar with some LLM post-training jargon and wants terms such as discriminative reward model or generative reward model explained when they first matter.

## Learning Preferences

- Calibrate known, unknown, and easily overlooked issues before giving a full answer.
- For basic cognition, prefer clear synthesis and experienced judgment from strong Zhihu answers, technical blogs, and explanatory material.
- For deep investigation, prioritize recent papers, technical reports, and model-company disclosures, normally from the previous 12 months.
- Focus on how leading model teams actually train or deploy systems when that information is public.
- Avoid over-researching settled basics or presenting a long source list without a clear decision role.
- Include important English source text directly in the answer, together with precise location and Chinese interpretation.
- Use only a small number of unusually helpful figures.

## Interaction Preferences

- The calibration response contains only these three headings: `我推测你已经知道`, `你当前可能缺少`, and `我发现你可能还需要关注`.
- The learner evaluates and corrects the calibration before deep research begins.
- The final answer should lead with the standard judgment, then supply only the background needed to understand it.

## Persistence Rule

Do not infer a lasting knowledge change from silence or a single question. Modify this profile only after an explicit request to persist a correction or preference.
