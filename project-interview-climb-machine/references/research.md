# Frontier Connection Research

## Scope before search

Ask the user which project angle they want to explore and whether to emphasize
academia, open source, industry, or a combination. Search only after reading the
minimal project base and optional role context. Avoid broad landscape surveys.

Default to two or three findings total, not two or three per source category.
Continue deeper only when the user chooses a direction.

## Source priorities

For academic claims, prefer:

1. original papers and official proceedings or preprints;
2. authors' official project pages and repositories;
3. relevant surveys or benchmarks when they directly clarify the project.

For open source, prefer the canonical repository. Do not equate stars with
technical quality. Consider maintenance recency, documentation, issues,
reproducibility, and credible adoption when relevant.

For industry claims, prefer:

1. official product documentation;
2. company engineering blogs, technical talks, and incident reports;
3. original benchmarks or case studies.

Use high-quality technical community posts and Zhihu to discover practical
debates, terminology, or Chinese-language explanations. Do not use them as the
sole support for a consequential technical claim when a primary source exists.

## Output

For each selected finding, provide:

- title and original link;
- publication or update date when available;
- one concise reason it is relevant;
- one question or thought direction the candidate may explore;
- a clear boundary between the source and what the candidate actually built.

Record the research date when saving current findings. Do not paste large
excerpts into the workspace. Do not automatically convert research into
interview wording, project facts, or claims about the candidate's past
knowledge.

## Relevance filter

Exclude an item when it is only thematically similar, famous, or easy to
name-drop. Prefer a source that clarifies the same problem, method, constraint,
evaluation issue, or credible next direction. Tell the user when no strong
connection was found instead of forcing one.
